Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect what we need to send you a massager, we share your address with the warehouse that ships it, and we do not sell your data to anyone. The long version follows.

Who is responsible

Unknot is operated by Indokalo AS, Nygardsvegen 36, 5419 Fitjar, Norway. We are the data controller for the information described here. Reach us at hello@unknot.store.

What we collect

  • Order details — your name, delivery address, email address and phone number if you give one
  • Payment confirmation — the amount, the currency and the last four digits of your card

We never see or store your full card number. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe.

Why we collect it, and on what basis

To take your order, get the massager to your door, answer you if you write, and keep the accounts our law requires. The legal basis is performance of a contract with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and for the bookkeeping, a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

Who we share it with

Three parties, and no others:

  • Stripe — processes the payment. Stripe is our payment processor and holds your card details, not us.
  • Our supplier and their carrier — we pass your name, address and phone number to the warehouse that packs and ships your order. That warehouse is in China. This is a transfer outside the EEA, and it is the only way an order can physically reach you. We send them nothing beyond what the parcel needs.
  • Our accountant — sees order totals as part of the books, as Norwegian law requires.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or hand it to advertisers.

How long we keep it

Order and accounting records are kept five years from the end of the financial year, because the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act says so. Emails you send us are deleted once the matter is settled and there is no reason to keep them.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to hello@unknot.store and we will answer within 30 days.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), or to the supervisory authority in your own country.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. When you go to pay, Stripe sets what it needs to run the checkout securely — that happens on Stripe's page, under Stripe's own privacy policy.
