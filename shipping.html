Shipping & Returns

Shipping

We offer free worldwide shipping on every order — no minimum purchase. Orders are processed within 1–2 business days.

MethodDelivery timeCost
Free Worldwide Shipping7–14 business daysFree

All orders include tracking, sent to your email once the parcel is on its way.

Returns

If it is not right for you, we accept returns within 30 days of delivery.

  • The item must be unused and in its original packaging
  • Email us first and we will send you return instructions
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after the return arrives
  • If the item arrived damaged or faulty, you do not need to return it — just send us a photo

Right of withdrawal (EU/EEA)

If you are buying as a consumer in the EU or EEA, you have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal from the day you receive the item, in addition to our own 30-day return policy. Contact us and we will handle it.
