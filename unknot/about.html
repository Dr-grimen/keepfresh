Our story

About Us

Unknot sells one thing: a shoulder massager that kneads instead of buzzing.

Why only one product

Most massage gadgets vibrate. Vibration is easy to build and easy to sell, and it does very little for shoulders that have been hunched over a desk since morning. The mechanism that actually helps is kneading — something pressing, rolling and letting go, the way thumbs do.

So we picked the one device we could find that does that properly, and we sell that. When something better comes along, we will sell that instead.

How we work

We ship worldwide from our supplier's warehouse, which is why delivery takes 8–14 days rather than two. In exchange, the massager costs a fraction of what the same mechanism costs on a shop shelf.

We would rather tell you that plainly than pretend there is a warehouse round the corner.

Straight answers

Unknot is a massager for relaxation and comfort. It is not a medical device, and we will not tell you it fixes anything. If something hurts in a way that worries you, see a doctor — not a gadget shop.
