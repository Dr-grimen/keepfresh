Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days and arrive in 8–14 days. Every order ships with tracking.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to most countries, and shipping is free on every order — there is no minimum purchase. If it turns out we cannot deliver to your address, we refund you in full, straight away.

How do I track my order?

You will get a confirmation email the moment you order, and a second email with your tracking number as soon as the parcel is on its way.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, if you contact us within 24 hours of ordering. After that the parcel is usually already on its way and we cannot stop it — but you can still return it.

What if it arrives damaged or does not work?

Email us with a photo and we will send a replacement or refund you in full. You do not need to send a broken unit back.

Does it need batteries?

Yes. The sealer takes standard batteries, which are not included in the box — use fresh ones, as weak batteries will not heat the strip properly.

How do I return something?

Email us within 30 days of delivery and we will send you return instructions. The item needs to be unused and in its original packaging.
