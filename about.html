About FreshSeal

FreshSeal is a small, independent shop with one product and one job: stop good food from going stale in a half-open bag.

Why one product

Most shops sell a hundred things badly. We would rather sell one thing well, describe it honestly, and stand behind it. The Mini Sealer is a simple heat sealer — you run it along the top of a bag and it closes airtight. That is all it does, and it does it well.

How we ship

Orders ship directly from our supplier to you, which is why delivery takes 8–14 days and why we can keep the price at $24.99 with free shipping. We would rather be upfront about that than pretend there is a warehouse behind us.

If something goes wrong

You have 30 days to return anything for a full refund, and if a unit arrives damaged we replace it without asking you to post it back. One person reads every email — write to us and you will get a real answer.
