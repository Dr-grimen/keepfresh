Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days and arrive in 8–14 days with tracking. You get the tracking number by email as soon as the parcel leaves.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship free to Europe, the United States, Canada, Australia and New Zealand. If your country is not offered at checkout, write to us and we will tell you honestly whether we can reach you.

How does it charge?

Over USB-C, with the cable in the box. A full charge lasts roughly a week of daily use.

Is it heavy?

It sits across your shoulders rather than hanging from your neck, so the weight spreads. Buyers describe it as noticeable when you pick it up and unnoticeable once it is on.

Can I use it while doing something else?

That is the point of the strap. It holds the massager in place so both hands stay free.

Is this a medical device?

No. It is a massager for relaxation and comfort. It is not intended to treat, diagnose or prevent any condition. If you have a medical concern, ask your doctor rather than us.

What if it does not suit me?

Send it back within 30 days of delivery and we refund you in full.
